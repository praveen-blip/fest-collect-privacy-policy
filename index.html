# Privacy Policy for FestCollect **Last Updated:** September 16, 2026 Welcome to **FestCollect** ("App", "we", "our", or "us"), operated by **DGSignz**. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when using our Android mobile application. By installing and using FestCollect, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. --- ## 1. Information We Collect ### A. Information You Provide Directly * **Account & Committee Information:** When a committee or administrator is onboarded or registers for access, we collect organizational details (e.g., Committee Name, Festival Type, Festival Year, Town/City, District, State) and administrator contact details (e.g., Name, Email Address, Mobile Number). * **Devotee & Contribution Records:** Treasurers and authorized committee members may enter devotee information (Name, Mobile Number, Address) and transaction details (Contribution/Donation Amount, Payment Mode, Date, Category, and Purpose). * **Expense & Operational Records:** Vendor expenses, vendor names, invoices/receipt notes, and daily financial closings entered by committee members. ### B. Automatically Collected Data * **Device & Usage Information:** We may collect device model, operating system version, app version, language, crash reports, and system performance metrics to ensure app stability. * **Advertising & Device Identifiers:** The app integrates **Google AdMob**, which automatically collects unique device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and interaction data to serve advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. --- ## 2. Device Permissions Used FestCollect requests only the permissions necessary to provide its core functionality: * **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required to sync festival records, receipts, and user authentication securely with cloud databases (Firebase). * **BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_CONNECT / BLUETOOTH_SCAN:** Used strictly to discover and connect to portable 58mm and 80mm ESC/POS thermal Bluetooth printers for instant receipt printing. We do not use Bluetooth for location tracking. * **CAMERA & STORAGE (Optional):** Used if you choose to attach expense receipts or committee photos. --- ## 3. Third-Party Services We Use We utilize industry-standard, secure third-party service providers that process data on our behalf: * **Google Play Services & Firebase (Google LLC):** * Firebase Authentication (User sign-in and security) * Cloud Firestore (Encrypted cloud database storage) * [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) * **Google AdMob (Google LLC):** * Serves banner and display ads to users. * [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) * [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543) --- ## 4. How We Use Your Information We use the collected information for: * Providing, managing, and maintaining festival accounts and receipt generation. * Generating thermal print receipts, PDF statements, and daily audit reports. * Secure multi-tenant cloud backup of committee accounts. * Serving non-intrusive banner ads through Google AdMob. * Providing technical support, responding to inquiries, and preventing unauthorized access. We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal or devotee data to any third-party data brokers or marketing firms. --- ## 5. Data Retention and Security * **Security:** We employ encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and multi-tenant security rules on our cloud databases to prevent unauthorized access. * **Data Retention:** Committee and financial records are retained as long as the committee maintains an active account to provide audit trail compliance. * **Account Deletion & Data Removal:** Committee administrators may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting our support team. Upon verified request, all corresponding user and committee records will be permanently purged within 30 days. --- ## 6. Children’s Privacy FestCollect is intended for use by adult festival committee organizers, treasurers, and volunteers. It is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. --- ## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. --- ## 8.
