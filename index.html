Fest Collect – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

Fest Collect is a festival contribution and committee management application designed for Ganesh, Durga Matha and other festival committees.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Address
  • Committee information
  • Contribution information
